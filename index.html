How Sciatica Develops and Why Treatment Matters

Sciatica typically starts when pressure builds on the sciatic nerve, often from a disc bulge, muscle tightness, or structural misalignment in the spine. The longer the irritation persists, the more it interferes with your ability to move freely. Addressing it early can help you avoid or delay more invasive options down the road.

Questions About Chiropractic Care for Sciatica

Is chiropractic care worth it for sciatica?
Many people find that spinal adjustments and decompression provide meaningful relief, especially when pain stems from misalignment or disc pressure. Results vary—some notice improvement quickly, while others benefit from a structured program over several weeks. The goal is to reduce your pain and restore function without relying on medication or surgery.

Should massage be part of sciatica treatment?
Yes, when the piriformis muscle or other surrounding tissues are tight, massage can ease compression on the nerve. Light, targeted pressure on the muscles around the sciatic pathway helps reduce inflammation and improve blood flow. This works best alongside other treatments rather than as a standalone solution.

When should I see a doctor instead of a chiropractor?
If your sciatica includes progressive numbness, severe weakness, or loss of bladder control, you need medical evaluation. For pain that's worsening despite rest or is caused by a structural issue like a major disc herniation, a physician should be involved. Dr. McNamara will tell you honestly whether chiropractic care can help or if you need a referral.
